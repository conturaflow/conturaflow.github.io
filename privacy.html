Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

1. Summary

The Contura Flow analytics tool ("the tool") reads statistics about the owner's own TikTok videos. It is used only by the account owner. It does not collect data about other people, and it does not share data with anyone.

2. Data the tool accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through TikTok's login and consent screen, the tool reads:

  • basic profile information for the owner's account (account ID, display name);
  • a list of the owner's videos and their metadata (ID, title or caption, publish date, duration);
  • aggregate statistics for those videos, such as views, likes, comments, shares, watch time, audience retention and traffic sources.

These statistics are aggregate counts that TikTok provides. They do not identify individual viewers. The tool does not read messages, contacts or other users' content.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to analyse how the owner's own content performs, for example which videos people keep watching and where they stop.

4. Storage and security

The data is stored on the owner's private computer. Access tokens are stored locally and are never published. Nothing is sent to third parties.

5. Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as it is useful for analysing the owner's content. The owner can delete it at any time. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app's security and permissions settings.

7. Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not collect data about children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
